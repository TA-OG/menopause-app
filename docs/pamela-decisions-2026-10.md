Three invitations across the week, not one practice a day. Every line comes from her
own edits. **Not shown to anyone until she approves the exact words.**

| # | Mel says | An invitation, if you'd like to |

| 1 | What you're feeling has a reason. You're not imagining it, and you're not broken. | Put your hands on your heart. Close your eyes and breathe. If it feels right, say: "My body is calm and my mind is calm too." |

- Day 7: "Take out love!" · "Mel keeps the world for you?" (she read "word" as "world"; dropped with day 7)
- Days 3 and 5 had no comment. They aren't in the three because she asked for at most three.

**Her "how do you feel now?" check** (comment on "Feeling like myself again"): *"we can
add some something like a liker scale. How do you feel now? And when we do that I want
women to close their eyes and breath. Then go inside and check- how do I feel instead

they leave the app they can rate that again. Maybe there's a breath pattern they can use
to pause for a short while. I think I'd need to do a video to talk that thought. … They
could write how that felt in the journal."*
→ Built as a pause-and-rate moment: close your eyes, breathe, check in, rate 1–5, and

## 6. Not part of the journey: Learn articles (doc 03) and the wellness review (doc 04)

**Learn articles** are written and published by Pamela in `/admin/articles`, and the
database is their source of truth. Her notes on the five drafts ("Ac1" [HbA1c], B12, folate,
ferritin; muscle loss shows before the scales move; strength training before walking;
"metabolism" wording; "fiber, fiber, fiber"; dropping progesterone cream for now) are
hers to apply there.

**Wellness review**: 50 comments on the plan's cards. They change health content in
`content/wellness/frameworks/*.yaml`, so every one has to meet the repo rule: a
verified, checkable source before it ships. Several go against current UK guidance
(NHS, NICE, BHF): saturated fat ("Theres nothing wrong with butter, red met or even full
fat diary"), salt ("Switch to mineral salts not sodium white table salt"), phytoestrogens
("This is a myth"), 30 g protein at a meal, avoiding all caffeine when fatigued, jumping and
HIIT for women with joint pain, adding digestive-enzyme supplements, and covid vaccines as
a cardiovascular risk factor. They need a separate review with sources, card by card.
**None of them is changed by the journey work.**
